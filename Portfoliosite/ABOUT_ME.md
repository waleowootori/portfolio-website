# Babawale Owootori

Babawale Owootori is a full-stack web developer, digital marketer, and designer based in Lagos, Nigeria.

He specializes in building modern, responsive, conversion-focused websites using a code-first approach. His workflow combines development, design, SEO, accessibility, performance, conversion optimization, and AI-assisted productivity to deliver websites faster without sacrificing quality.

He builds complete web experiences from concept to deployment. His process includes planning layout structure, visual direction, and user experience with tools like Canva and other web design resources, then translating those concepts into production-ready code using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and modern front-end tools.

AI coding tools play a major role in his workflow. He uses them to accelerate development tasks such as scaffolding components, debugging, refactoring, improving responsiveness, optimizing SEO structure, and speeding up repetitive coding work. Rather than depending entirely on AI, he uses it as a productivity amplifier while still making the technical and design decisions himself.

Because of his background in digital marketing, he pays close attention to performance, user experience, accessibility, and conversion optimization. He thinks beyond visuals and focuses on building websites that support business goals.

His professional positioning combines design sense, coding ability, digital marketing awareness, and AI-assisted execution.
